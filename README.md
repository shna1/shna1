# Hi, I'm Shabar Naderi

I am a financial data analyst focused on Python, quantitative research, machine learning and Iranian financial markets. I build reproducible research projects that turn market data into transparent models, portfolio analysis and decision-support tools.

## Areas of focus

- Financial data analysis and visualization
- Quantitative research and time-series modeling
- Portfolio analytics and asset allocation
- Machine-learning applications in financial markets
- Research dashboards and reproducible Python workflows

## Selected projects

### [Iranian Gold ETF Direction Modeling](https://github.com/shna1/iran-gold-etf-research)

Time-aware machine-learning research on the next-day direction of an Iranian gold ETF, with chronological validation, transaction-cost-aware backtesting and explicit interpretation limits.

### [Commodity Signal Lab](https://github.com/shna1/commodity-signal-lab)

A responsive Plotly Dash research dashboard for commodity valuation, rule-based signals and transaction-cost-aware backtesting.

### [Gold Derivatives & Arbitrage Analysis](https://github.com/shna1/gold-derivatives-arbitrage-analysis)

Scenario-based comparison of Iranian gold funds, bullion certificates and futures, including capital requirements, basis calculations and arbitrage analysis.

### [Iran Asset Portfolio Analytics](https://github.com/shna1/iran-asset-portfolio-analytics)

Portfolio analytics and long-only asset allocation across Iranian equities, the USD/IRR market and gold coins using Python and SciPy.
